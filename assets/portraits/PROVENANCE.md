# Host portrait cutouts

`amit-original.png` and `jason-original.png` were extracted unchanged from the hosts' DragonFly accelerator presentation. The source photos were supplied for that presentation. `amit-cutout.png` and `jason-cutout.png` were created from those portraits by removing the background so they could sit over the presentation gradients. The cutouts are edited derivatives, not pixel-identical crops of the originals. Both files were visually checked and have transparent PNG backgrounds.

The edit request was limited to removing the white circular and charcoal outer backgrounds while preserving each person's face, hair, expression, pose, clothing and framing. No new body parts, shadow or surrounding shape were requested.
