# Host-mark provenance

- `durham-university.png` was rasterised from a [Durham University PDF logo](https://www.maths.dur.ac.uk/users/s.m.fearn/assets/img/DU-logo.pdf) on the university's `dur.ac.uk` domain. The presentation crops this source image to show the crest alone.
- `dragonfly-mark-soft-black.svg` came from the hosts' DragonFly brand kit. `dragonfly-mark-soft-black.png` is its raster export for PowerPoint.
- The OpenAI mark is `assets/tool-logos/openai.png`; its source is recorded in [the tool-mark note](../tool-logos/PROVENANCE.md).

The About Us slide displays the Durham crest, OpenAI mark and DragonFly mark together. Their appearance does not imply an endorsement of this workshop by any of those organisations.
